# visualising-uncertainty-train-delay-predictions
Data and materials accompanying the paper and extended abstract "Visualising Uncertainty in Train Delay Predictions: Effects on User Trust, Comprehension, and Decision Quality"

## Overview

This repository contains the study materials, datasets, analysis resources, and supplementary documents used in the research project investigating how different uncertainty visualisations influence user trust, comprehension, and decision-making in train delay prediction interfaces.

The project compares multiple uncertainty visualisation approaches and evaluates their effects through expert interviews and a user study.

## Research Objective

Train delay predictions are inherently uncertain and must communicate probabilistic information effectively to support traveller decision-making. This project investigates how different visual representations of uncertainty affect:

* User comprehension
* Perceived trustworthiness
* Cognitive effort
* Decision quality

The study focuses on non-expert users in time-critical travel scenarios.

## Methodology

The research was conducted in two phases:

### 1. Expert Interviews

Two rounds of semi-structured interviews with HCI and UI/UX experts were conducted to identify suitable uncertainty visualisation concepts and derive design recommendations.

### 2. User Study

A comparative user study evaluated six different visualisation approaches for communicating train delay uncertainty.
